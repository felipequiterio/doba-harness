Transfer and Adjustment), "Hohmann Transfer", pp. 72-74. Method summary.

Hohmann Transfer (two-burn transfer between two coplanar circular orbits)

A Hohmann transfer moves a spacecraft between an inner circular orbit of radius
r1 and an outer circular orbit of radius r2 using an elliptical transfer orbit
that is tangent to both. The transfer ellipse has its periapsis at r1 and its
apoapsis at r2, so its semi-major axis is

        a_t = (r1 + r2) / 2

Two impulsive burns are required:

  Burn 1 (at r1): raise the speed from the inner circular speed to the speed of
  the transfer ellipse at its periapsis.
  Burn 2 (at r2): raise the speed from the transfer ellipse's apoapsis speed to
  the outer circular speed.

Building-block formulas:

  - Circular-orbit speed at radius r:
        v_c = sqrt( mu / r )

  - Speed on any orbit at radius r, given semi-major axis a (vis-viva):
        v = sqrt( mu * (2/r - 1/a) )

The two burn magnitudes are the speed differences at each tangent point:

        dv1 = v(r1, a_t) - v_c(r1)        [at the inner radius]
        dv2 = v_c(r2)    - v(r2, a_t)     [at the outer radius]

and the total cost of the transfer is

        dv_total = dv1 + dv2

where mu is the gravitational parameter of the central body, r1 and r2 are the
inner and outer circular radii, and a_t is the transfer-ellipse semi-major axis.
The same two-burn structure works for an inward transfer (r2 < r1); the burns
are decelerations but the formula dv_total = dv1 + dv2 is unchanged.

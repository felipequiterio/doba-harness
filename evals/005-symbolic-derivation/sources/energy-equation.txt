Energy Equation

The specific kinetic energy of the orbiting body is

        KE = v^2 / 2 = (r_dot)^2 / 2 + (r * theta_dot)^2 / 2        (2.15)

From Exercise 2.6, we obtain the (very important) Energy Equation:

        E = v^2 / 2 - mu / r = - mu / (2 * a)                       (2.20)

where a denotes the semi-major axis of the conic-section orbit, v is the speed
at radius r, and mu is the gravitational parameter of the central body.

An alternate form of the Energy Equation is

        ( (r_dot)^2 + (r * theta_dot)^2 ) / 2 - mu / r = - mu / (2*a)   (2.21)

E is the "specific mechanical energy (of the satellite)." We usually just call
it the "energy."

Remarks:
  1. The energy E is independent of the eccentricity e. That is, the energy
     depends on the size of the orbit, but not on the shape of the orbit.
  2. Fix r. As v increases, the energy increases and, therefore, a increases.
